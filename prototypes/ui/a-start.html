How BenchUp works

Every project moves through six stages
1
Design

Add boards from your stock or draw parts, and size them exactly.

2
Joinery

Join touching parts with mortises, dowels or dovetails and check every fit.

3
Materials

Assign species and sheet goods, grain direction and the finish.

4
Cut list

Get cut sizes, board layouts and shop drawings for the workshop.

5
Planning

Follow the build step by step, with methods that fit the tools in your workshop.

6
Estimate

Price the work and materials, then send the client a quote.
